# Mailing list introduction (send to dev@iggy.apache.org)

Subject: Introduction: Apache Iggy DevRel support

Hi everyone,

I wanted to introduce myself properly to the Apache Iggy community. I have started a new part-time role with LaserData, working on developer relations across Apache Iggy and the broader LaserData open-source and open-core ecosystem.

For Iggy, my role is to help the project and its community grow in useful, sustainable ways. The areas I expect to focus on first are contributor onboarding, documentation, examples, integrations, issue and PR triage support, and helping more developers understand where Iggy fits for high-performance streaming, Rust, and real-time data infrastructure.

I am starting by spending time with the codebase, website, issues, discussions, and existing community material so I can understand where I can be most helpful before proposing changes. I will keep any concrete work visible and bring drafts or recommendations back for community review rather than assuming direction.

I would welcome input on where you think DevRel help would have the most impact. In particular:

- Where do new contributors or users currently get stuck?
- Which docs, examples, or integrations would be most useful to improve first?
- Are there recurring questions from Discord, GitHub, or the mailing list that deserve better public answers?
- Are there community processes that would benefit from more written guidance?

Thanks, and I am looking forward to working with you all.

Justin
